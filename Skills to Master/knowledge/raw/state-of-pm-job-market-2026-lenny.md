# State of the Product Job Market in Early 2026

**Source:** https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/state-of-the-product-job-market-in-ee9  
**Publication:** Lenny's Newsletter  
**Date:** March 24, 2026  
**Date ingested:** 2026-04-25  
**Data source:** TrueUp, 9,000+ companies globally

## Key Findings

- 7,300+ open PM roles globally — highest since 2022, 75% above 2023 low, +20% since Jan 2026
- 67,000+ engineering openings worldwide (26K in US alone)
- AI roles: hockey-stick growth — both AI companies and AI-specific roles at traditional tech firms
- Design roles: flat (~5,700 globally) — PM-to-designer demand ratio now 1.27x (flipped from 2023)
- Bay Area: 23% of PM roles (up 50% since 2022), 1/3 of all AI roles
- Global hubs: NYC (#2), Bengaluru, London, Tel Aviv, Singapore
- Recruiter hiring approaching 2022 peak — leading indicator of sustained demand
- Remote work: declining (paywalled detail)
- Caveat: more openings ≠ faster hiring for individuals
