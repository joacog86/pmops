# The Definitive Guide to Mastering Product Sense Interviews

**Source:** https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/the-definitive-guide-to-mastering
**Author:** Ben Erez (Guest post on Lenny's Newsletter)
**Publication:** Lenny's Newsletter
**Date:** April 1, 2025 | **Ingested:** 2026-04-25

## Overview

Ben Erez (founder, PM at Meta, first PM at multiple startups) draws on interviewing 50+ candidates and coaching hundreds of students. Five-step framework for product sense interviews.

## Key Framework: Five Essential Components

### 1. Clear Communication (3-5 minutes)
- Make 2-4 focused assumptions about scope (role, geography, platform)
- Articulate a clear game plan before diving in
- Use "waypointing" to signal transitions between sections
- Check in with interviewer before proceeding
- Recommended opening: "Before I dive in, I'd like to walk you through my plan for our time together..."

### 2. Product Motivation (3-5 minutes)
- Product description and value: connect features to deeper human needs
- Strategic context: competitive positioning and company strategy alignment
- Mission statement: create a purpose-driven North Star
- Move beyond surface descriptions — not "helps users share photos" but "enables people to maintain meaningful connections through visual storytelling"

### 3. Segmentation (8-10 minutes)
- Ecosystem analysis: map all stakeholders, select one group
- Segment creation: break audiences into distinct groups based on motivations and behaviors — NOT demographics
- Prioritization: reach vs. underserved-degree framework
- Persona development: vivid, specific person embodying key characteristics
- Critical: segments must be mutually exclusive

### 4. Problem Identification (8-10 minutes)
- User journey mapping: visualize how persona interacts with product in specific contexts
- Problem discovery: identify friction points with emotional specificity
- Prioritization: rank by frequency and severity, connect to mission
- Key distinction: Problems = obstacles ("struggle to find relevant plants for limited lighting"); Needs = desires ("want beautiful flowers")

### 5. Solution Development (8-10 minutes)
- Brainstorming: generate 3+ meaningfully different approaches, not variations
- Prioritization: impact vs. effort framework
- V1 definition: outline concrete implementation with user discovery strategy
- Risk mitigation: identify potential challenges and mitigation approaches

## Evaluation Dimensions

Five dimensions — excellence in one cannot compensate for weakness in others:
1. Clear communication with solid structure
2. Product motivation and mission understanding
3. Audience segmentation and prioritization
4. Problem identification and ranking
5. Solution development and v1 planning

## Common Pitfalls

**Clear Communication:** thinking out loud without structure; asking interviewer for direction; prematurely narrowing solution space

**Product Motivation:** focusing only on features; vague or too-specific mission statements; directions misaligned with company strengths

**Segmentation:** demographics-only; skipping ecosystem analysis; non-mutually-exclusive segments

**Problem Identification:** confusing needs with problems; generic journeys applicable to any product; jumping to solutions

**Solution Development:** rushing to single solutions; feature lists rather than problem-solving; solutions that don't leverage company capabilities

## Practice Recommendations

- Create standard templates for assumptions and game plans
- Build muscle memory through 20-30 practice questions
- Practice journey visualization ("day in the life" scenarios)
- Explicitly test segment mutual exclusivity
- Generate diverse solutions within 5-minute brainstorms
- Time yourself in each section

## Critical Success Factors

Strong candidates demonstrate: systematic thinking about product ecosystems, clarity on ambiguous problems, empathy from behavioral context, creativity leveraging company strengths, clear communication, flexibility responding to interviewer feedback.
