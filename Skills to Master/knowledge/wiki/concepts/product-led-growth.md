# Product-Led Growth (PLG)

**Type:** Concept / Go-to-market strategy

## Definition

A growth model where the product itself is the primary driver of acquisition, activation, retention, and expansion — rather than sales or marketing motions. Users experience value before (or without) talking to a human.

## Core mechanism

The product does the selling. Free trials, freemium, self-serve onboarding, and in-product virality replace or complement traditional top-of-funnel. The bet: if users get to value fast, they convert, expand, and refer.

## PLG vs. Sales-Led Growth

| | PLG | SLG |
|---|---|---|
| Primary driver | Product experience | Sales relationship |
| Entry point | Free trial / freemium | Demo / call |
| Time to value | Hours to days | Weeks to months |
| CAC | Lower (self-serve) | Higher (human touch) |
| Works best when | Product is intuitive, value is obvious | Complex, high-ACV, enterprise |

## Things being debated in the field

- PLG + Sales hybrid ("PLG-assisted sales") is becoming the dominant model — pure PLG is rare at enterprise scale
- "Product-Qualified Accounts" (PQAs) as a bridge between PLG and sales handoff
- Whether PLG works in complex B2B or only in bottoms-up SaaS

## Contradictions / open questions

- PLG requires significant product investment upfront — not always viable for early-stage startups
- Freemium can cannibalize paid conversion if not designed carefully
