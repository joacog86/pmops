# Ecosystem Growth
**Type:** Concept / Strategy

## Definition
Ecosystem growth is a company-wide GTM strategy where growth comes primarily through intermediaries — partners, creators, communities, integrations — who already have access and trust with your target audience, rather than going directly to prospects through owned channels.

The core insight (Emily Kramer, 2025): AI has democratized access to every traditional growth channel simultaneously. When everyone can produce content at scale, run AI SDRs, and ship products faster, traditional channels (inbound, outbound, virality, events, lifecycle) converge to table stakes. The differentiator is the ecosystem — the people and companies you can't easily copy.

## The flywheel
Ecosystem partners create and distribute content → you amplify and repurpose → greater reach and credibility → new customers and partners → cycle strengthens.

Unlike a linear funnel, this compounds: each new partner makes the ecosystem more valuable to the next one.

## Types of ecosystem partners

| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Creators / influencers | LinkedIn voices, Substack writers, YouTube channels |
| Channel partners | Agencies, consultants, resellers |
| Integration partners | Products that serve the same user |
| Communities | Discord servers, professional forums, trade associations |
| Customer advocates | Power users who speak publicly about the product |

## Partner selection: Coverage vs. Composition
Two filters (from advertising) for evaluating partner quality:
- **Coverage:** % of your total target audience the partner reaches — high coverage = broader TAM reach
- **Composition:** % of their audience that overlaps with yours — high composition = less waste

High on both = ideal match. High on only one is still useful but requires a different activation strategy.

## Win-win-win as the bar
Every partnership must create value for three parties: you, the partner, and the prospect. Two out of three is not enough — it creates imbalance that kills the relationship over time. Value doesn't have to be financial: shared data, early access, event co-hosting, and distribution all count.

## Implementation (5 steps)
1. **Not a side project** — needs senior buy-in and product team involvement for integrations and partner-specific features
2. **Map your ecosystem** — complement research (who has your audience's trust?), not just competitive research
3. **Win-win-win test** — before launching any partnership
4. **Start narrow** — pick one program, prove it, then expand
5. **Combine channels** — ecosystem amplifies existing channels; it doesn't replace them

## Why it matters now
Three structural shifts make ecosystem more important:
1. AI made content and outreach abundant → trust and relationships are scarce
2. GTM teams are leaner → ecosystem is contracted leverage at high ROI
3. Product virality is harder → too many options, too much noise
