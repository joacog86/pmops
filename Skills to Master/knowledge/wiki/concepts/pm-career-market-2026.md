# PM Career Market — 2026

**Type:** Concept / Market context  
**Last updated:** 2026-04-25

## State of the market

Recovery is real but uneven. 7,300+ open PM roles globally (highest since 2022), but demand is concentrated in:
- **AI-adjacent roles** — AI PM, PM at AI companies, PM for AI features at traditional tech
- **Senior/Lead level** — junior PM market remains compressed
- **Bay Area geography** — 23% of all PM roles, and growing

## What companies are hiring for in 2026

Based on market signals across sources:

### Must-haves (table stakes for Senior PM+)
- AI fluency — not just using AI tools, but thinking in AI product terms: what can be automated, what the model failure modes are, how to instrument AI features
- Demonstrated impact with metrics — "I shipped X and it moved Y by Z%"
- Cross-functional leadership — owned the room, not just contributed to it
- Technical depth — enough to earn engineering trust, not to write code

### Differentiators for PM Lead
- Built and led a team (even informal — intern, APM, contractor)
- Operated at strategy + execution simultaneously — not just one or the other
- External signal — published, spoke, known in the field
- AI agent / automation fluency — can design systems, not just use tools

### What's declining in value
- "I ran sprints and wrote tickets" — execution-only PM profiles are getting squeezed
- Generic portfolio without outcomes — case studies without metrics
- Pure design-thinking PM profiles — design is getting automated faster

## The AI PM angle specifically

The fastest-growing PM specialty. Two flavors:
1. **PM at an AI company** — building the AI product itself (Anthropic, OpenAI, Cursor, etc.)
2. **AI PM at a traditional company** — owns the AI layer being added to an existing product

Both require: ability to reason about model behavior, define evals, navigate "good enough" vs. "not yet ready," and communicate uncertainty to non-technical stakeholders.

## What this means for Joaco specifically

- AI fluency: already building — this PM OS is evidence
