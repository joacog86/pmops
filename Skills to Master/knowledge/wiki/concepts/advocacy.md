# Advocacy

**Type:** Concept / Growth lever

## Definition

The stage where a Champion user actively promotes the product to others — through reviews, referrals, case studies, or organic word-of-mouth. In the PLG Flywheel, advocacy closes the loop by converting Champions into sources of new Strangers.

## Forms of advocacy

- Public reviews (G2, Capterra, App Store)
- Case study participation
- Personal recommendations (word of mouth, LinkedIn)
- Feature suggestions that shape the roadmap
- Inviting teammates (viral / product-led)

## Key insight from PLG Flywheel

Champions don't always advocate spontaneously — they need to be asked. The framework is explicit: "Champions think your product is special — so be sure and let them know the feeling is mutual." Advocacy needs to be activated, not just waited for.

## Embedded advocacy (design pattern)

Some products bake advocacy into the product itself:
- Drift's "Chat by Drift" widget on free tier — every conversation is an impression
- Calendly's meeting invites — every recipient sees the brand
- Notion's shared pages — viewer becomes a potential user

This is advocacy at zero marginal cost — no CS effort required.

## Metrics to watch

- NPS (sentiment proxy, not a guarantee of action)
- Review velocity on third-party platforms
- Referral rate / viral coefficient
- PQA generation from existing customers inviting colleagues
