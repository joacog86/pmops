# Activation

**Type:** Concept / Growth metric

## Definition

The moment a user internalizes that the product solves their problem. Also called the "aha moment" or "time-to-value." The transition from Explorer to Beginner in the PLG Flywheel.

## Why it matters

Activation is the hinge of retention. Users who don't activate churn before they have a chance to become regulars. All acquisition effort is wasted if activation fails.

## Common mistakes (per PLG Flywheel source)

- Feature tours instead of goal-oriented onboarding — showing everything instead of the one thing the user needs
- Optimizing for breadth (many features touched) vs. depth (one core value felt)
- Measuring login or signup as activation — these are proxies, not the real thing

## How to define your activation moment

1. Identify what "regular users" do that churned users don't
2. Find the earliest signal that predicts long-term retention
3. Make that the target of your onboarding flow

## Examples

- Slack: sending a message to a teammate (not just signing up)
- Dropbox: saving a file and seeing it sync across devices
- Notion: creating and sharing a page
