# BRAC System

## Definition

A two-part career documentation framework developed by Deepali Vyas. BRAC stands for **Biggest wins, Results with numbers, Accomplishments others witnessed, Contributions above your level.**

The system has two distinct tools that serve different purposes — most people conflate them or only know one:

- **BRAC File** — a collection of external evidence that your work matters to others. Think professional testimonials: emails from clients saying you saved a deal, Slack messages from colleagues saying you made the difference, performance notes, thank-yous, any written documentation. Screenshot it, forward it, archive it.
- **BRAC Book** — a living quantifiable impact document, updated 2–3 entries per week in real time. Tracks: revenue influenced, costs reduced, headcount led, projects shipped, timelines compressed, problems solved with dollar figures attached.

BRAC File = social proof. BRAC Book = business case. Both are required — walking into a promotion conversation with only one is like showing up to an interview with half a resume.

## Origin

Developed by Deepali Vyas from 25+ years of executive search and observing what made the strongest candidates credible in promotion and compensation conversations. Introduced across [[sources/deepali-vyas-career-power]] (The Elite Edge newsletter, 2025–2026). The "Brag Book" concept has existed in generic career advice, but Deepali's contribution is: (1) the distinction between social proof and quantified business case as two separate tools, and (2) the weekly update cadence as the practice that makes it work.

## Key claims

- BRAC File = testimonial archive. External evidence you can't produce on demand — it has to be captured in real time. — cited from [[sources/deepali-vyas-career-power]]
- BRAC Book = quantifiable impact record. Measurable, defensible, specific to business outcomes. Update 2–3 entries per week, before you forget. — cited from [[sources/deepali-vyas-career-power]]
- Both are required for promotion conversations. The file provides social proof ("here's what others think"). The book provides the business case ("here's what I actually delivered"). — cited from [[sources/deepali-vyas-career-power]]
- Most people cannot articulate their own impact at review time because they never tracked it in real time. The BRAC Book solves this. — cited from [[sources/deepali-vyas-career-power]]
- The BRAC Book is also the foundation for converting illiquid career assets — making internal reputation portable outside the org. — cited from [[sources/deepali-vyas-career-power]]

## Contrasts with

- **Brag Book (generic concept)** — the generic "brag book" is typically just a testimonial/feedback archive (= Deepali's BRAC File). The BRAC Book (quantified impact record) is her original addition. Most career advice only mentions the testimonial layer.
- **STAR format** — STAR captures how you tell a story in an interview; the BRAC Book captures what raw material you have to draw from. They're complementary: BRAC Book is the raw data, STAR is the packaging.

## Open questions

- No external validation data cited on whether BRAC Book users get promoted at higher rates — this is practitioner advice from one expert, not empirically validated methodology.
- The "2–3 entries per week" cadence assumes low-friction capture. If your work is highly unstructured (strategy work, ambiguous projects), defining what counts as a "win" with numbers is its own skill.
