# ProductLed.org

**Type:** Entity / Organization  
**URL:** https://www.productled.org  
**Focus:** Product-led growth education, frameworks, and community

## What they produce

Frameworks, articles, courses, and a community around PLG strategy. The PLG Flywheel is their flagship framework. Associated with Wes Bush (author of "Product-Led Growth: How to Build a Product That Sells Itself").

## Perspective / bias

Advocates for PLG as the dominant go-to-market motion. Content is practitioner-oriented and generally pro-PLG — balanced against sales-led approaches, but the framing favors self-serve and product-first growth. Methodology is grounded in interviews with real companies (50+ for the Flywheel framework).
