# Product School

**Type:** Entity — Educational Institution / Publication
**Website:** https://productschool.com
**CEO:** Carlos Gonzalez de Villaumbrosia

## What it is

Product School is a PM education platform offering certifications, bootcamps, events (ProductCon), and a blog. It's one of the most widely-read PM content sources alongside Lenny's Newsletter and Mind the Product.

## Credibility signal

High distribution, practitioner-sourced content. Blog covers frameworks, metrics, and interview prep. Certifications include PM, AI Product Management, and AI Evals. Less opinionated than Lenny's — more reference-oriented.

## Bias to note

Commercial interest in positioning PM frameworks as complex enough to require paid training. Some content is surface-level. Filter for frameworks with real-world examples and practitioner attribution.
