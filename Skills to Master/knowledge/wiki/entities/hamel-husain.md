# Hamel Husain
**Type:** Entity / Author

## Who he is
Hamel Husain is a machine learning engineer with 20+ years of experience. He co-created (with Shreya Shankar) the AI Evals for Engineers & PMs course on Maven — the #1 highest-grossing course on the platform, with 2,000+ students and adoption at OpenAI, Anthropic, and other leading AI labs.

## Why he's in this wiki
His Lenny's Newsletter guest post (September 2025, co-authored with Shreya Shankar) is the most rigorous, practitioner-tested treatment of how to build eval systems that actually drive product improvement — as opposed to eval dashboards that get ignored. It operationalizes Aman Khan's "why evals matter" into a concrete 3-phase process.

## Bias to note
Heavy ML/engineering orientation. The open/axial coding methodology and TPR/TNR framing are rigorous but assume some statistical literacy. For PM audiences, the most actionable parts are error analysis (phase 1) and the flywheel concept (phase 3) — not the statistical correction formulas in the appendix.
