# Lenny's Newsletter

**Type:** Entity / Publication  
**URL:** https://www.lennysnewsletter.com  
**Author:** Lenny Rachitsky (ex-Airbnb PM)  
**Audience:** PMs, founders, growth practitioners

## Perspective / bias

Practitioner-first, data-grounded, generally optimistic about product craft. Heavy Airbnb/Silicon Valley lens. Content quality is high but skews toward consumer/PLG/growth-stage companies — less useful for enterprise or early-stage B2B contexts. Guest posts vary in rigor.

## Why relevant

One of the highest-distribution PM publications. Sets norms and vocabulary for the field. What appears here tends to become "received wisdom" among PMs within 6-12 months.
