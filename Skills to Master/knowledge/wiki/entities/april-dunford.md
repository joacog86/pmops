# April Dunford
**Type:** Entity / Author

## Who she is
April Dunford is widely considered the world's leading expert on B2B product positioning. She has worked with 300+ B2B technology companies over 10 years and is the author of *Obviously Awesome* (positioning) and *Sales Pitch* (positioning for sales decks). She runs a positioning consultancy and publishes on Substack.

## Why she's in this wiki
Her guest post in Lenny's Newsletter (March 2026) is the most practical, pattern-based treatment of B2B positioning failures available. It goes beyond the basics of *Obviously Awesome* to address the four specific roadblocks teams hit in practice.

## Bias to note
Commercial interest: sells positioning workshops and consulting. Her frameworks are practitioner-tested but are designed to be teachable and repeatable — which sometimes oversimplifies edge cases (e.g., product-led growth companies where positioning is more emergent than deliberate).

## Key frameworks
- **5 components of positioning** (*Obviously Awesome*): competitive alternatives → differentiated capabilities → value for buyer → target market → market category
- **4 advanced roadblocks** (Lenny's 2026): competitor disagreement, product pessimism, undefined value, unclear what you're positioning
