# Hilary Gridley
**Type:** Entity / Author

## Who she is
Hilary Gridley is a product builder and writer with a background in behavior change products (WHOOP, Dropbox, Big Health — a digital depression treatment). She created "30 Days of GPT" in 2024 (featured in Harvard Business Review) and updated it in 2026 as Couch-to-5K for AI, a progressive 30-day daily practice program.

## Why she's in this wiki
Her Lenny's Newsletter guest post (April 2026) is the most grounded, behaviorally-informed take on AI adoption available — backed by direct experience building products that change behavior (fitness tracking, digital therapeutics). Her insight that "busy = procrastination" and that AI fluency is a habit problem, not a knowledge problem, reframes how to think about team AI adoption.

## Bias to note
Her program (couchto5k.ai) is a commercial product — the post doubles as marketing. The behavior change principles are solid and evidence-based; the specific 30-day program is one implementation of them, not the only one.
