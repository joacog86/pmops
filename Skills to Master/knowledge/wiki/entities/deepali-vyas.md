# Deepali Vyas

## What it is

Executive recruiter at ZRG Partners with 25+ years in executive search. Creator of "The Elite Edge" newsletter (eliterecruiter-newsletter@mail.beehiiv.com, via Beehiiv), published weekly with career strategy content. Former corporate leader who owned a $50M+ P&L before transitioning to executive search. Claims 50,000+ interviews conducted. Builds a media platform (mentioned in her own newsletter) alongside the search practice.

## Why it matters

Deepali's perspective is distinct from the academic/framework-heavy interview coaches in this wiki (Ben Erez, Erika Gemzer): she speaks from inside the hiring machine — as the recruiter who runs searches, sits in comp committee conversations, and watches candidates succeed or fail over decades. Her advice is practitioner-first, sometimes blunt, and rooted in observing patterns across hiring decisions at scale. Particularly strong on: the political dynamics of job searching while employed, how promotion decisions really get made, and negotiation as a multi-window practice (not a single event).
