# Emily Kramer
**Type:** Entity / Author

## Who she is
Emily Kramer is a B2B marketing executive and advisor. She was the first Head of Marketing at Asana and has held marketing leadership roles at several B2B startups. She writes a newsletter on B2B marketing and advises startups on GTM strategy.

## Why she's in this wiki
Her guest post in Lenny's Newsletter (November 2025) is the clearest articulation of ecosystem as a systematic growth strategy for B2B startups in the AI era — framing it not as a collection of tactics but as a compounding flywheel that makes every other channel more effective.

## Bias to note
Strong B2B SaaS / startup lens. Her examples (Supabase, Clay, Lovable, Vercel) skew toward developer-first or PLG-adjacent products where community and open-source ecosystem dynamics are particularly strong. The framework is more work to apply in traditional enterprise B2B with complex sales cycles.
