# Noam Segal
**Type:** Entity / Author

## Who he is
Noam Segal is AI Insights Manager at Figma and former UXR leader at Zapier, Airbnb, Meta, Twitter, Intercom, and Wealthfront. He has co-authored four collaborations with Lenny Rachitsky, including the AI interview coach post and this large-scale AI productivity survey (1,750 respondents).

## Why he's in this wiki
Two distinct contributions in this wiki:
1. **AI interview coaching** ([[sources/ai-interview-coach-lenny-noam-segal]]): "spiky POV" differentiation framework + free Claude Code coach setup
2. **AI productivity survey** ([[sources/ai-productivity-survey-noam-segal-lenny]]): largest independent survey on AI's actual impact on tech worker productivity; data on adoption by role, tool PMF, and the upstream gap

## Bias to note
Survey audience skews toward engaged Lenny's subscribers — likely more AI-forward than the average tech worker. ROI numbers are probably higher than industry average. The tool rankings are a snapshot and shift fast.
