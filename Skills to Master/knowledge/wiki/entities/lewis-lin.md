# Lewis Lin

**Type:** Entity — Author / PM Interview Coach
**Known for:** *Decode and Conquer* (PM interview bible), CIRCLES Method, STAR Method variants
**Website:** https://www.lewis-lin.com

## What he's known for

Lewis Lin wrote *Decode and Conquer*, one of the most cited PM interview prep books. He created the CIRCLES Method as a structured approach to product design questions — now widely used in PM interview prep globally.

## Credibility signal

High influence in PM interview prep space. CIRCLES is the most widely-taught product design framework in interview coaching contexts. Referenced by Product School, Exponent, and most PM interview prep resources.

## Bias to note

Commercial interest in PM interview prep (books, courses). Frameworks optimized for interview performance — may be more rigid than what's needed in actual product work.
