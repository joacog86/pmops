# Vikas Kansal
**Type:** Entity / Author

## Who he is
Vikas Kansal leads product for Google AI subscriptions — the team responsible for monetizing Gemini, NotebookLM, and the Google AI subscription bundle. He has been on the front lines of figuring out AI monetization at massive scale (hundreds of millions of DAUs).

## Why he's in this wiki
His Lenny's Newsletter guest post (May 2026) is the most concrete, insider-sourced treatment of AI monetization available. He writes from direct experience building and iterating Google's AI paywall — not from theory.

## Bias to note
Consumer AI at Google scale. His examples (Gemini, NotebookLM, Genie 3) are B2C subscription products with very high DAUs. The framework applies to B2B AI products but the conversion dynamics (multi-turn metric, cross-platform trigger) need adaptation for enterprise buying cycles and seat-based pricing.
