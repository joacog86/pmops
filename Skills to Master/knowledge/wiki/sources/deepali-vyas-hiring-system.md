# How the Hiring System Actually Works — Deepali Vyas (The Elite Edge)

**Fecha:** 2025-09 – 2026-05 (4 issues) | **Fuente:** Email newsletter (eliterecruiter-newsletter@mail.beehiiv.com)

**Procesos:** `interview-prep` `stakeholder-alignment`

## TL;DR

Most candidates prepare the wrong way — memorizing stories when interviewers are actually auditing judgment. From a 25-year executive recruiter (50,000+ interviews): the hiring system is rigged toward networks and referrals, not applications; you're being evaluated before you apply; and when your boss suspects you're leaving, you have a narrow window to protect your process without lying or damaging your integrity.

## Core idea

**The interview is a judgment audit, not a performance.** At the executive level, no one hires your answers — they hire your judgment. Elite candidates don't just answer the question; they answer what's behind the question.

**The 5 interview questions that reveal judgment:**

| Question | What's really being evaluated | Framework |
|---|---|---|
| High-stakes decision under ambiguity | Risk judgment, speed vs. precision, ownership | Set stakes → Show ambiguity → Walk decision logic → Own outcome |
| Disagreeing with leadership | Emotional maturity, influence, business alignment | State decision → Share perspective without ego → Show influence → Land alignment |
| Self-awareness as a leader | Coachability, ability to scale | Be specific → Show real feedback → Demonstrate adjustment → Tie to outcomes |
| Why this role, why now | Intentionality, market awareness, strategic fit | Anchor to their inflection point → Connect experience → Show why now |
| Failure or misstep | Ownership, pattern recognition, growth | Be direct → Own it → Show what changed → Demonstrate improved judgment |

**The 3 recruiter types — know who you're talking to:**

- **Corporate (in-house)**: Driven by time-to-fill metrics. Real work happens through internal referrals, not online applications. Navigate by becoming an internal referral; optimize LinkedIn for ATS; don't take silence personally.
- **Contingent (external, paid per hire)**: Fast-moving, goal is speed. Be responsive — ghosting kills chances. Communicate clearly on salary, location, timeline.
- **Retained/Executive Search**: Hunt passive talent, build long-term trust. Every conversation is an interview. Stay in touch even when the role isn't a fit — relationships matter here on a multi-year horizon.

**ATS reality check:** The ATS is not access — it's optics. Real hiring happens in networks, referrals, and conversations you're not yet part of. Become an internal referral, circulate your profile to 5 target recruiters per month, and make your LinkedIn read as "value and relevance" — not "open to work."

**When your boss thinks you're a flight risk:** The question "Are you happy here?" is threat assessment — not genuine curiosity. Three wrong answers exist (panic denial, honest admission, deflection). The right move is to redirect toward your growth without lying or handing over leverage. 

Scripts:
- *Early exploring:* "I'm always thinking about my development. Right now I'm focused here — but I'd love a bigger conversation about the path forward. Can we schedule time?"
- *Mid-process:* "I'm always open to learning what the market looks like, but I'm not in a position to walk away from something I'm building. What I'd love to talk about is [promotion/comp/scope]."
- *Near offer:* "I don't have anything to announce. But I do think it's worth having a real conversation about where I'm headed here." → Then accelerate your timeline.

After the conversation, watch for behavioral shifts: removed from projects, skip-level check-ins, HR reaching out "just to connect" — these are signals to move faster, not wait.

## Cuándo aplicarlo

- Before any interview: shift prep from "polish stories" to "build perspective on what's behind each question"
- When engaging with recruiters: use the 3-recruiter model to calibrate how formal, fast, or relationship-oriented each conversation should be
- When your manager asks if you're happy: apply the flight risk scripts to protect your timeline and leverage without damaging the relationship

## Qué significa para Joaco

For the N26 process: the "why this role, why now" framework maps directly — anchor to N26's fintech inflection point (embedded finance, AI-led KYC), connect your B2B SaaS discovery experience, show why this is the logical move now. The judgment audit framing means prep should be perspective-based (what you think about problems) not story-based (what you once did). The retained search lens applies if N26 is using a search firm — every call is an interview even before the formal loop.

- **vs. [[sources/first-round-interview-erika-gemzer]]** — Gemzer's STAR++ is about story *structure*; Deepali's framework is about what the interviewer is *actually evaluating*. Complementary: use Gemzer to structure the answer, Deepali to understand the hidden question.

## Watch out

- The 5-question framework is exec-level focused (Director/VP+). For PM roles, adapt: "high-stakes decision under ambiguity" maps well, but the P&L framing needs to become "product outcome and tradeoff" framing.
- The flight risk advice assumes you have leverage (an active process in play). If you don't have alternatives, the calculus changes — more transparency earlier may be warranted.
