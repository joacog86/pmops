# PLG Flywheel — ProductLed.org

**Type:** Article / Framework  
**Source:** https://www.productled.org/foundations/the-product-led-growth-flywheel  
**Org:** ProductLed.org  
**Date ingested:** 2026-04-25  
**Methodology:** Interviews with 50+ companies across sizes and business models

## TL;DR

Replaces the linear AARRR funnel with a cyclical flywheel where product experience drives acquisition. The core insight: satisfied users become advocates who bring in new strangers, creating compounding growth. Each stage of the flywheel has distinct user profiles, needs, and team ownership.

## The 5 Stages

| Stage | User transition | Key question | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evaluate | Stranger → Explorer | Does this solve my problem? | Marketing + Sales |
| Activate | Explorer → Beginner | When do they get value? | Product |
| Adopt | Beginner → Regular | Is it habit yet? | Product + CS |
| Expand | Regular → Champion | What else can it solve? | Product + CS + Marketing |
| Advocate | Champion → (new Strangers) | Will they tell others? | CS + Marketing |

## Key Claims

- 81% of customers visit 3+ websites and research pricing before buying — Evaluate stage is high-stakes
- Activation = "time-to-value" — the moment users internalize that the product solves their problem
- Adoption = habit, not just usage — user reaches for the product without deliberate decision (Slack example)
- Champions need prompting to advocate — they won't always act unprompted; ask for reviews, case studies, beta participation
- The flywheel spins faster as each stage compresses — compounding effect

## Nuances by product type

- **Multi-player (Slack):** Evaluate compresses, Activate-Adopt-Expand creates a micro-flywheel per new invitee → generates PQAs (Product-Qualified Accounts)
- **Viral loop (Drift):** Free users skip Expand and go straight to Advocate via the "Chat by Drift" widget attribution
- **Two-sided marketplace (Wise):** Suppliers skip Evaluate entirely — they're pulled in by the buyer, forced to Activate → creates involuntary advocates

## Tensions / Things to interrogate

- Framework assumes product quality is high enough to generate genuine champions — doesn't address what happens when it's not
- "Company-wide commitment" framing is aspirational; in practice ownership is often contested between product and CS
- PQA concept is useful but underspecified — what signals define a PQA varies heavily by product
