# State of the Product Job Market in Early 2026 — Lenny's Newsletter

**Type:** Market data / Industry report  
**Source:** https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/state-of-the-product-job-market-in-ee9  
**Publication:** [[entities/lennys-newsletter]]  
**Data source:** TrueUp (9,000+ companies globally)  
**Date:** March 2026 | **Ingested:** 2026-04-25

## TL;DR

PM market is recovering strongly — 7,300+ open roles, highest since 2022. AI roles are the growth driver. Bay Area concentration is increasing. Design is flat. Remote is declining. Despite good numbers, individual job searches remain hard.

## Key data points

| Metric | Number | Trend |
|---|---|---|
| Open PM roles globally | 7,300+ | +20% since Jan 2026, 75% above 2023 low |
| Open engineering roles | 67,000+ | Accelerating |
| Open design roles | 5,700 | Flat since 2023 |
| PM roles in Bay Area | 23% of total | Up 50% since 2022 |
| AI roles in Bay Area | 33% of total | Concentrated |

## What this signals

- **AI PM is the hottest positioning right now** — both pure AI companies and "AI PM at traditional tech" are growing fastest
- **Design is stagnating relative to PM** — AI is eating design faster than it's eating PM (yet)
- **Bay Area concentration is a real dynamic** — if you're not there, you're competing for a shrinking share of remote/distributed roles
- **Recruiter hiring as leading indicator** — hiring pipeline is growing, sustained demand ahead

## Tensions / things to interrogate

- "7,300 open roles" is a global number — role quality, seniority, and location vary enormously
- More openings ≠ faster hiring for individuals; competition has also increased
- Bay Area concentration may be a selection effect (AI companies cluster there) not a signal that non-Bay Area PMs are at a disadvantage everywhere
- Design flatness could be cyclical, not structural — hard to conclude AI is "replacing" design from one data point

## Implications for PM career positioning (2026)

1. **AI fluency is not optional** — AI roles growing at all companies, not just AI-first
2. **PM Lead / Senior PM roles are the target** — market is recovering but at senior level (juniors still getting squeezed)
3. **Bay Area presence or remote-first companies** — where you play matters
4. **Specialization beats generalism** — "AI PM," "Growth PM," "Platform PM" are clearer targets than generic PM
